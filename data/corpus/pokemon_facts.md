# Pocket Monsters Quick Facts

## Pikachu Highlights
- Pikachu is an Electric-type Pokémon known for storing electricity in its cheeks.
- The species evolves into Raichu when exposed to a Thunder Stone.
- Pikachu is recognized as the mascot of the Pokémon franchise.

## Eevee Curiosities
- Eevee has an unstable genetic makeup that allows multiple evolutions, including Vaporeon, Jolteon, and Flareon.
- Its evolutions are influenced by elemental stones, friendship, time of day, and even specific locations in later games.

## Kanto Trivia
- The Kanto region is the setting of the original Pokémon Red and Blue games.
- Professor Oak gives new trainers their first Pokémon in Pallet Town.
- The legendary birds Articuno, Zapdos, and Moltres each represent ice, electric, and fire elements.
